Boundaries come from the City of Chicago's own ArcGIS service and the open data portal, clipped to East Lakeview, then tested against your address point in the browser. Search covers Diversey to Irving Park, the lake to Racine, because that is the extent of the data loaded here.

Responsibility notes are drawn from CDOT, Streets and Sanitation, Water Management, the Park District and the ward office. Programmes and prices change. Treat the links as the authority and this as the map of where to look.
